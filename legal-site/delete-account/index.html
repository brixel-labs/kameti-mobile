Delete your account

Last updated: November 11, 2026

You can delete your Kameti account and its data yourself, in the app, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Kameti and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will happen, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and your profile.
  • Any draft kametis you had not started.
  • Your saved people (“My people”) and their contact details.
  • Your link to every kameti you joined: you can no longer see them.

What stays, and why

  • Kametis you joined: your name and the payments recorded for you stay in that owner’s records, because they are part of the group’s shared history. Your email address and phone number stay only if the owner entered them; ask the owner, or us, to remove them.
  • Past kametis you ran: they stay for their members, with your name shown as “Deleted account”.

If you run a kameti

You can’t delete your account while a kameti you run is still active, because other people’s payments are still being recorded in it. Finish or close it first; the app shows which ones are blocking.

Can’t use the app?

Email kameti@fhbtechservices.com from the email address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm it is you, then delete it within 30 days.
