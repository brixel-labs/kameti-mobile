Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Kameti is an app for keeping the records of a rotating savings group (a kameti or committee): who pays, how much, and who receives each month. It is made by Brixel Labs Inc. (“we”, “us”). No money moves through Kameti: it only keeps records, and it never asks for bank or card details.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead). You can change your display name in the app.
  • Kameti records an owner enters. The kameti’s name and settings; members’ names and, optionally, their email address and phone number; slots and shares; payments and payouts (amounts, dates, method and notes); and notes about the kameti.
  • Invitations. The email address an owner invites, and whether the invite was sent, delivered, accepted or declined.
  • Activity history. A log of changes (for example “payment recorded”) so everyone can see what happened and when.
  • On your device only. Preferences such as light or dark appearance and notification switches.

We do not use advertising, we do not sell personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see what

  • The owner of a kameti sees all of its records, including the contact details they entered.
  • A member who joins sees their own dues and payments, the payout schedule, and notes the owner chooses to share. Other members see your name, never your email address or phone number.
  • Nobody outside a kameti can see anything in it.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show each person the right records, to send invitations and service emails, to keep the activity history, and to keep the service secure and working. We may contact you about your account or this policy.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Data is stored in Singapore.
  • Resend: sending invitation emails.
  • Apple and Google: signing in with your Apple or Google account.
  • Vercel: hosting this website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. See how account deletion works for what is removed and what stays.

A kameti’s records belong to its owner’s ledger. If you were a member, your name and the payments recorded for you stay in that owner’s records after you delete your account, so the group’s history stays correct. Your sign-in and your link to those records are removed. To ask an owner to remove your contact details, or for any other request, email kameti@fhbtechservices.com.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each person read the records they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Kameti is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will update this page, and its date, before the app starts collecting anything new (for example, push notifications).

Contact

Questions or requests: kameti@fhbtechservices.com. Brixel Labs Inc., Canada.
