What Kameti is

Kameti is a record-keeping tool for rotating savings groups. It helps an owner record who has paid, what is owed and who receives each month, and lets members see their own records. Kameti does not hold, move or guarantee money, and it is not a bank, lender or payment service. Every payment happens outside the app, between the people in the group.

Your group’s agreement

The arrangement between the people in a kameti (amounts, order, what happens if someone pays late or stops paying) is between them. We are not a party to it and are not responsible for disputes, missed payments or losses between members.

Accuracy of records

The owner is responsible for what they record. The app calculates balances from those records; if a record is wrong, the results will be too. Mistaken payments are corrected by voiding them, and every change is kept in the activity history.
